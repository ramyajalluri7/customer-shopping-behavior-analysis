# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview
An exploratory data analysis project focused on understanding customer shopping behavior, purchasing patterns, subscription trends, discount usage, and customer ratings.

The goal is to turn customer transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support better marketing, customer engagement, and business decisions.

## Business Objectives
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Analyze customer spending behavior.
- Evaluate subscription trends.
- Examine discount usage.
- Understand customer satisfaction through ratings.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer engagement and retention.
